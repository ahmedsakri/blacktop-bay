This notice explains the information used by Blacktop Bay, a browser racing game from AppsOverFlow.

Records stored on your device

Your selected car and circuit, sound setting, analytics choice, best race time, drift score, race-credit balance, per-car upgrade levels, per-car body colours and paint finishes, and recent race reward IDs are stored in your browser. Race records are separated by car and circuit. The game does not require a player account, name or email address. Browser storage can also contain replay data from previous versions; the current race displays computer-controlled opponents.

Use Privacy → Clear race records in the game to remove saved race records. This keeps your upgrade levels, credit balance and paint choices. Clearing this website’s browser data removes its settings and workshop progress as well. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still play; settings, records, upgrades, credits and paint choices will last only for the current page session.

Optional gameplay analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded through Google Tag Manager only after you choose Allow analytics. Choosing Essential only keeps these tags from loading. You can change your decision through the game’s Privacy button.

When enabled, analytics measures page views, car and circuit selections, opening the garage, race starts and completions, laps, pauses, resumes, car resets and nitro use. Event details can include the chosen car or circuit, race position, duration, drift score and reset count. We do not send your name, email address, raw keystrokes, saved replay or local race history. Page addresses sent by the game exclude query strings and fragments; referral details are limited to the referring origin.

Google can process technical request information, including browser and device information and approximate location. Analytics cookies may be set when you opt in. Turning analytics off stops new game events, disables this game’s measurement and removes its accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase events already sent to Google.

Advertising and Google services

Blacktop Bay is being submitted for Google AdSense review. The current game includes publisher verification information but does not load ad-serving code or display advertisements. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalisation are denied in the current analytics setup. Any future advertising integration will require an update to this notice and the relevant consent controls.

You can read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner websites.

Hosting and external links

Firebase Hosting delivers the game and can process ordinary technical request logs, including network and device information needed to serve and protect the website. Links to AppsOverFlow and Google open services with their own privacy practices.

Contact AppsOverFlow

For a privacy question, use the AppsOverFlow contact page. Please describe the issue without including passwords or other sensitive account information.
