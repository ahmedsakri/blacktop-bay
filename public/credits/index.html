Real-car model collection

These 33 car representations across 16 brands use individually credited artists’ geometry. We preserve the represented model identity and do not present a road model as a different racing variant. Each source is adapted for interactive browser use; handling, upgrades and Nitro are independently authored arcade mechanics.

Circuit outlines and arcade adaptations

The Grand Prix venue pack derives geographic outlines from f1-circuits by Tomislav Bacinger, copyright © 2019–2025, under the MIT License. The original data is unofficial. AppsOverFlow scales, smooths and widens the outlines for compact arcade racing, relocates starting grids where necessary and authors a non-crossing reinterpretation of Suzuka.

Official venue names, lengths and the calendar were checked against Formula 1’s 2026 calendar on 1 October 2026. The pack includes 23 current-calendar venues and Sakhir and Jeddah as original-calendar bonuses, alongside nine original Camber Reign courses. Playable lap lengths differ from the real circuits; elevation, banking and surveyed detail are not reproduced.

Camber Reign is an independent arcade game. These adaptations are not official Formula 1 assets, licensed circuit replicas or an endorsement by Formula One, the FIA or the venues.

Recorded engine textures

Selected cars blend short licensed recordings with Camber Reign’s original synthesis. These are adapted sound textures, not recordings of each represented model or measured RPM libraries. Uncovered cars retain their procedural voices. Nitro, tyres, impacts and the Liquid Lines lobby soundtrack remain original synthesis.

Ferrari 355 engine compartment

ferrari355underhood4.mp3 by enginemusic, copyright 1997 as noted by the recorder, under CC BY 3.0. Recorded inside a Ferrari 355 Spider with intake and valve-cover microphones. Adapted as an arcade texture for the 458 Spider and selected V8 cars; not their original engine recording.

Porsche 911 street recording

porsche_911.wav by mharo, under CC0 1.0. The source specifies a Porsche 911 in Barcelona; its year and variant are unknown. Used as an adapted texture for the 930 Turbo and 911 GT3.

Mustang idle

Ford Mustang Engine (1985).wav by lmbubec, under CC0 1.0. A short idle section supplies a restrained low-rev texture for the Mercedes-AMG GT and Maserati MC Stradale; it fades as revs rise.

Aston Martin acceleration

Acceleration Aston Martin by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, under CC0 1.0. The source does not identify the model. Its use on the One-77 is an authored adaptation.

Changes: selected segments, mono conversion, 24 kHz resampling, filtering, DC correction, loop overlap, seam smoothing and amplitude normalization. Freesound derivatives use the public HQ MP3 previews; the Aston Martin derivative uses the source WAV. No creator or manufacturer endorsement is implied.
