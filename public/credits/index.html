Blacktop Bay is created by AppsOverFlow. Its world, racing systems and presentation combine original work with the credited contributions below.

Real-car model collection

These 16 car representations across 13 brands use individually credited artists’ geometry. We preserve the represented model identity and do not present a road model as a different racing variant. Each source is adapted for interactive browser use; handling, upgrades and Nitro are independently authored arcade mechanics.

McLaren 570S Coupé

Original model by MMC Works (Márcio Meireles), under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Prepared separate desktop and mobile meshes; preserved authored body surfaces and UVs, separated the four wheel assemblies, adjusted named rubber, glass and paint materials, reduced texture dimensions and compressed the delivery files.

McLaren Senna

Original model by LiteBeet, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Removed the studio staging; normalized scale and orientation, retained authored body normals and UVs, separated the four wheel assemblies and rubber/alloy materials, corrected archived paint, glass and interior materials, and prepared compressed desktop and mobile meshes.

McLaren P1 GTR

Original model by ALIEEEN, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Removed staging/people where present. Baked world transforms, normalized scale and +Z forward/+Y up coordinates. Preserved authored normals/UVs; separated whole wheel components into articulated pivots. Conservatively simplified secondary geometry with normals/UV weights, corrected named material roles where archived viewport shading was unsuitable for PBR, resized embedded textures and Meshopt-compressed for delivery.

Circuit outlines and arcade adaptations

The Grand Prix venue pack derives geographic outlines from f1-circuits by Tomislav Bacinger, copyright © 2019–2025, under the MIT License. The original data is unofficial. AppsOverFlow scales, smooths and widens the outlines for compact arcade racing, relocates starting grids where necessary and authors a non-crossing reinterpretation of Suzuka.

Official venue names, lengths and the calendar were checked against Formula 1’s 2026 calendar on 1 October 2026. The pack includes 23 current-calendar venues and Sakhir and Jeddah as original-calendar bonuses, alongside nine original Blacktop Bay courses. Playable lap lengths differ from the real circuits; elevation, banking and surveyed detail are not reproduced.

Blacktop Bay is an independent arcade game. These adaptations are not official Formula 1 assets, licensed circuit replicas or an endorsement by Formula One, the FIA or the venues.

Promotional artwork

The coastal racing campaign image is an AI-generated promotional illustration, created for AppsOverFlow using a staged render of the credited McLaren P1 GTR, McLaren Senna and Ferrari 458 Spider models as a visual reference. It is not a gameplay screenshot. The loading-screen background is a separate staged render of those models. Actual race and garage captures are labelled separately on AppsOverFlow.
