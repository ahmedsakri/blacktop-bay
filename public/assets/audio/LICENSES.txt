The CC0 and CC BY entries below permit redistribution and adaptation under their
listed terms. The separately marked Sonniss GDC entries are licensed for
incorporation in this game and retain additional distribution restrictions.
Each bank was modified by AppsOverFlow: selected source intervals, mono
24 kHz PCM16 conversion, 65 Hz high-pass / 5.8 kHz low-pass filtering,
DC removal, cosine loop overlap, endpoint correction and amplitude
normalization. Runtime pitch, rev bands, gain and load EQ are authored.
Retain the relevant title, author, source, license and modification notice
when redistributing a CC BY derivative. No recorder or manufacturer
endorsement is implied. Freesound source previews are not original masters; Sonniss sources are the
original studio WAVs. Raw studio takes and attached images are not shipped.

original synthesis; Liquid Lines remains the only lobby music.
