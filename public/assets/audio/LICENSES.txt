Camber Reign — recorded engine texture sources
Provenance and exact source intervals: ENGINE-SOURCES.json
Derivative loop banks: engines/*.wav
Original sources checked 2 October 2026. GranTurismo and AMG sources checked 3 October 2026.

All sources below permit redistribution and adaptation under CC0 or CC BY.
Each bank was modified by AppsOverFlow: selected source intervals, mono
24 kHz PCM16 conversion, 65 Hz high-pass / 5.8 kHz low-pass filtering,
DC removal, cosine loop overlap, endpoint correction and amplitude
normalization. Runtime pitch, rev bands, gain and load EQ are authored.
Retain the relevant title, author, source, license and modification notice
when redistributing a CC BY derivative. No recorder or manufacturer
endorsement is implied. Source previews are not original recording masters.

All 33 game cars have a selected recorded texture. Family/model adaptation
is explicit; recordings were not made of every exact game model. Electric
Rimacs use an electric Tesla recording, never a combustion source. W16,
flat-12 and specialized hybrid race engines use disclosed proxies. Actual
source RPM and microphone placement are stated only where documented.
The original synthesis remains a fallback for blocked/failed decoding.
Nitro, impact, tyre layers and the Liquid Lines lobby soundtrack remain
original synthesis; Liquid Lines remains the only lobby music.
