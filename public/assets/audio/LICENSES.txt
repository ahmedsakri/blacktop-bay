Original describes a Ferrari 355 Spider underhood recording, with one
microphone by the intake and one by the valve cover. The redistributed
file is a derivative of the publicly available HQ MP3 preview. Modified
by AppsOverFlow: selected segments, mono downmix, resampling, filtering,
DC and loop-seam correction, overlap and amplitude normalization.

All banks are adapted sound textures for an arcade mix. The game's car
mappings are proxies: none of these banks is claimed to have been recorded
from the exact represented game model. Playback speed and normalized-rev
bands are authored, not measured RPM. Where no suitable bank is assigned,
Camber Reign retains its original procedural voice. Nitro, impact and tyre
layers and the Liquid Lines lobby soundtrack remain original synthesis.
