Adapted for Blacktop Bay by AppsOverFlow, 2026.
The Three.js Draco edition was decoded; geometry was welded and simplified
with glTF Transform and meshoptimizer into two browser-oriented detail levels.
The approximately 150,332- and 63,781-triangle editions retain named nodes,
source materials and surface normals. The files require no runtime decoder.
The original model contained no image textures.

The Apex GT, Apex Sprint, Torque R and Torque RS in-game builds further adjust scale, orientation and materials, animate
wheel assemblies, and add original fixed racing roofs, roll cages, aero and liveries. These game
variants, their names and handling are fictional. They are based on the
credited Ferrari 458 Italia mesh, not claimed to be wholly original models.
