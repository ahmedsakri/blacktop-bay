Prior adaptation by the APEX / filan214 project: regrouped the original geometry
into sixteen named display assemblies, cut illustrative seams through fused
bodywork, assigned red and graphite materials, removed uniform vertex colours,
and baked orientation. This is a concept surface, not official team CAD or a
complete mechanical assembly.

Blacktop Bay preparation: welded duplicate vertices; simplified each assembly
with meshoptimizer through glTF Transform v4.5.1; retained all sixteen named
nodes, source attribution and license metadata. High version: 146,054 triangles
(relative error limit 0.003). Low version: 60,348 triangles (limit 0.008).
No textures or real racing-team liveries were added. Runtime scale/orientation,
materials and wheel pivots are adjusted for the game. The Vortex P1 and
Vortex X builds use this geometry with fictional names, handling and liveries.
