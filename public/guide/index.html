How to complete a Blacktop Bay race

Pick a car and circuit, start from the grid, and complete three laps through the checkpoints. These six steps cover the controls and race finish.

  1. Choose a race car and optional upgrades

    Open Garage and choose one of twenty race builds across GT, Formula and Prototype families. All are available immediately. Open Performance to spend earned race credits on Engine, Tyres, Nitro or Handling, or start with the stock car.

  2. Choose a circuit and start

    Select Harbor Flow, Dockyard Technical, Coast Run, Summit Switchback or Bay Grand Prix on the home screen, then choose Race Now or Race This Car. Wait for the three-second countdown; all four drivers start together.

  3. Steer into the corner

    Acceleration is automatic on every device. Drag left or right across the race view to steer and lift your finger to center the steering, or use Left and Right or A and D on a keyboard. Use measured inputs to follow the bend. Down or S is an optional keyboard brake; braking overrides acceleration and nitro.

  4. Drift through a corner

    Turn sharply at speed to drift automatically, then ease the steering back into line to bank a clean drift. Keyboard players can also hold Space for the optional handbrake. Contact can lose unbanked points.

  5. Use nitro on a clear straight

    Hold Shift or the Nitro button to boost. Nitro is the only on-screen driving button; acceleration is automatic. Release Nitro to recharge while driving, with faster recharge during clean drifts. Keyboard braking or the optional handbrake interrupts boost. Release an empty trigger before trying to boost again.

  6. Complete three laps and read the results

    Follow the circuit through its ordered checkpoints for three laps. The finish screen shows your position, time and earned race credits while remaining rivals continue to their own finish. Choose Race Again, Garage or Home to continue.

Your car. Five levels further.

Every car has five upgrade levels each for Engine, Tyres, Nitro and Handling. From Garage, choose a car and open Upgrades. Levels improve real driving performance and remain installed for later races when browser storage is available.

You start with 1200 race credits, shared across the garage. Each component’s five levels cost 200, 400, 700, 1100 and 1600 credits. All cars and circuits are available from the start; credits cannot be bought with real money.

A valid three-lap finish pays 900 / 650 / 500 / 350 credits for first through fourth place. Earn one extra credit for every 20 drift points, capped at 500 bonus credits per race. Each race pays once; a run longer than 15 minutes earns no credits.

Upgrade levels are separate for each car, and progress is saved in this browser rather than a player account. Clear race records keeps your workshop; clearing site data removes credits and upgrades. If storage is blocked, new progress lasts only for the current page session.

Frequently asked questions

What is Blacktop Bay?

Blacktop Bay is a free single-player 3D browser racing game by AppsOverFlow. Choose from twenty race builds from GT, Formula and Prototype families and thirty asphalt circuits, then race three race opponents over three laps using drifting and rechargeable nitro. Earn race credits to upgrade your cars.

Is Blacktop Bay free, and do I need an account?

Yes. Blacktop Bay is free to play without a game account or app installation. All twenty race builds and all thirty circuits are available from the start. Optional car upgrades use earned race credits, with no real-money payments.

Is Blacktop Bay multiplayer?

No. One human player races three computer-controlled drivers: Mira, Jax and Nova. The four-car field is a single-player race, not online multiplayer or ghost playback.

Which race cars can I choose?

Choose from twenty fictional race builds: Apex GT, Apex Sprint, Torque R, Torque RS, Vortex P1, Vortex X, Spectre LM, Spectre LM-R, Cinder R, Cinder RX, Kestrel GT-R, Mirage LMP, Solstice One, Tempest XR, Corsair GTS, Stratus GT3, Vector F, Zenith FX, Vela R and Aurora LMX. The garage spans GT, Formula and Prototype designs, with different acceleration, handling, speed limits and nitro tuning. The builds combine shared licensed model geometry with original coachwork and aero; they are not twenty independently sourced models. Every build supports its own upgrades and free paint choices.

How many circuits and laps are there?

There are thirty circuits and every race lasts three laps with you and three rivals. Five courses are original Blacktop Bay designs. The Grand Prix pack adds compact adaptations of 23 current 2026 calendar venues, including Sepang, plus Sakhir and Jeddah as original-calendar bonuses. The calendar was checked on 1 October 2026. Arcade lap lengths differ from the real venues; Suzuka uses a non-crossing adaptation.

How do I use and recharge nitro?

Acceleration is automatic. Hold Shift or the Nitro button to boost; Nitro is the only on-screen driving button. Release it to recharge while driving; clean drifts recharge it faster. Stock tanks range from 2.8 seconds in Apex Sprint to 4.0 seconds in Stratus GT3. Each Nitro upgrade adds 0.35 seconds, for 1.75 extra seconds at level 5, and improves thrust and recharge. Keyboard braking overrides acceleration and nitro, and holding the optional handbrake interrupts boost. After the tank empties, release the control before using it again.

Can I play Blacktop Bay on a phone?

Yes, on a phone browser that supports WebGL 2. Race in landscape with automatic acceleration. Drag left or right across the race view to steer, then lift your finger to center the steering. Turn sharply at speed to drift automatically and hold Nitro to boost. Nitro is the only on-screen driving button. Rotate upright to pause; rotate back and choose Keep driving to resume. The menu and garage also work in portrait. Graphics performance depends on the device and browser.

How do I finish a race and save my result?

Complete three laps in the correct direction through the ordered checkpoints. Your result shows finishing position, race time, best lap, drift points, resets and earned race credits; rivals receive times when they actually finish. Best time and drift score are stored locally for each car-and-circuit combination when browser storage is available. Runs longer than 15 minutes do not create records or earn credits.

Can I play offline?

No offline mode is provided. An internet connection is needed to load the game, and Blacktop Bay does not install an offline cache. Once loaded, the racing simulation runs in your browser.

How do I recover or pause?

Press R or select Reset car to return to your last valid road position. Press Esc or P, or use the pause icon, to pause. Switching away from the tab pauses an active race. Resetting does not refill nitro or award free lap progress.

Can I upgrade every car?

Yes. Each car has five purchasable levels for Engine, Tyres, Nitro and Handling. Open Garage, choose a car and open Upgrades. The five level costs are 200, 400, 700, 1100 and 1600 race credits per component. Engine improves acceleration, tyres improve grip and braking, handling improves steering response and stability, and nitro improves boost capacity, thrust and recharge. All four also raise the speed ceiling. Levels are saved locally for each car and apply to later races when browser storage is available.

Can I change my car’s colour and finish?

Yes. Open Garage, choose a car and select Paint. Choose one of 10 curated colours or Team original, then select Gloss, Metallic or Satin. All paint is free and changes appearance only. Choices are saved separately for each car in this browser when storage is available; otherwise they last for the current page session. Clearing site data removes them.

How do I earn race credits, and where is progress saved?

You start with 1200 shared race credits. A valid three-lap finish awards 900 credits for first, 650 for second, 500 for third or 350 for fourth, plus one credit per 20 drift points up to a 500-credit bonus. Each race pays once. Your balance and per-car upgrades stay in this browser when storage is available; they do not sync to other devices. Clearing race records keeps workshop progress, while clearing site data removes it. If storage is blocked, new credits and upgrades last only for the current page session.

Browser and privacy notes

Use a browser with WebGL 2 graphics enabled. The game needs an internet connection to load. Switching away pauses an active race; reduced-motion preferences remove decorative camera motion while retaining movement needed to drive. The speaker button controls sound.
