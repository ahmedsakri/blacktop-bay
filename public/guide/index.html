How to complete a Blacktop Bay race

Pick a car and circuit, start from the grid, and complete three laps through the checkpoints. These six steps cover the controls and race finish.

  1. Choose a race car and optional upgrades

    Open Garage and choose one of ten race builds across GT, Formula and Prototype families. All are available immediately. Open Upgrade car to spend earned race credits on Engine, Tyres, Nitro or Handling, or start with the stock car.

  2. Choose a circuit and start

    Select Harbor Flow, Dockyard Technical, Coast Run, Summit Switchback or Bay Grand Prix on the home screen, then choose Race Now or Race This Car. Wait for the three-second countdown; all four drivers start together.

  3. Steer and brake before corners

    The car accelerates automatically. Use Left and Right or A and D to steer, and Down or S to brake. On a phone, use the corresponding touch pads. Brake before tight corners and use short steering inputs.

  4. Drift through a corner

    Turn into a corner and briefly hold Space or the Drift pad. Release it and gently steer back into line to bank a clean drift. Contact can lose unbanked points.

  5. Use nitro on a clear straight

    Hold Shift or the Nitro pad for extra acceleration and speed. Release it to recharge while driving, with faster recharge during clean drifts. Release an empty trigger before trying to boost again.

  6. Complete three laps and read the results

    Follow the circuit through its ordered checkpoints for three laps. The finish screen shows your position, time and earned race credits while remaining rivals continue to their own finish. Choose Race Again, Garage or Home to continue.

Pick your stretch of asphalt

Harbor Flow

About 1.22 km per lap. Waterfront sweepers lead into a twisting inland section. Start here to practise braking, short drifts and clean exits.

Dockyard Technical

About 1.53 km per lap. A longer sequence of dockside turns rewards precise steering. Save nitro for clear straights between the tighter sections.

Coast Run

About 1.13 km per lap. Open coastal curves provide longer opportunities to accelerate. Plan each boost for a clear stretch of road.

Summit Switchback

About 1.66 km per lap. Technical asphalt winds through rocky, pine-lined switchbacks. Brake early and link the direction changes with controlled steering.

Bay Grand Prix

About 1.53 km per lap. A wide permanent circuit combines a long pit straight with flowing chicanes. Use the extra road width to plan a clean exit and an overtaking line.

Every circuit uses a three-lap race with one human driver and three race opponents.

Covered stands put spectators behind the barriers on every circuit, with a larger grandstand and pits at Bay Grand Prix. Four numbered grid bays line up beneath the start/finish gantry; its countdown lights signal the race start above the full-width checkered stripe.

Your car. Five levels further.

Every car has five upgrade levels each for Engine, Tyres, Nitro and Handling. From Garage, choose a car and open Upgrades. Levels improve real driving performance and remain installed for later races when browser storage is available.

ComponentWhat improves
EngineAcceleration and speed ceiling

You start with 1200 race credits, shared across the garage. Each component’s five levels cost 200, 400, 700, 1100 and 1600 credits. All cars and circuits are available from the start; credits cannot be bought with real money.

A valid three-lap finish pays 900 / 650 / 500 / 350 credits for first through fourth place. Earn one extra credit for every 20 drift points, capped at 500 bonus credits per race. Each race pays once; a run longer than 15 minutes earns no credits.

Upgrade levels are separate for each car, and progress is saved in this browser rather than a player account. Clear race records keeps your workshop; clearing site data removes credits and upgrades. If storage is blocked, new progress lasts only for the current page session.

Your colour. Your finish.

Open Garage → Paint to preview 10 curated colours or restore Team original, with Gloss, Metallic or Satin finishes. Paint is free and purely visual. Each car keeps its own choice when browser storage is available; otherwise the choice lasts for the current page session.

Race the field. Reach the finish.

Mira, Jax and Nova drive visible, solid cars. They negotiate corners, attempt passes and can make contact with your car. Complete three laps in the correct direction through the ordered checkpoints; shortcuts, travelling backwards and resetting do not award free laps.

Your results show position, race time, best lap, drift points and resets. Rivals still racing receive their actual times when they finish. Your fastest time and best drift score are saved locally for each car and circuit when browser storage is available; runs over 15 minutes do not create records or earn race credits.

Frequently asked questions

What is Blacktop Bay?

Blacktop Bay is a free single-player 3D browser racing game by AppsOverFlow. Choose from ten race builds from GT, Formula and Prototype families and five asphalt circuits, then race three race opponents over three laps using drifting and rechargeable nitro. Earn race credits to upgrade your cars.

Is Blacktop Bay free, and do I need an account?

Yes. Blacktop Bay is free to play without a game account or app installation. All ten race builds and all five circuits are available from the start. Optional car upgrades use earned race credits, with no real-money payments.

Is Blacktop Bay multiplayer?

No. One human player races three computer-controlled drivers: Mira, Jax and Nova. The four-car field is a single-player race, not online multiplayer or ghost playback.

Which race cars can I choose?

Choose Apex GT, Apex Sprint, Torque R or Torque RS from the GT family; Vortex P1 or Vortex X from the open-wheel Formula family; or Spectre LM, Spectre LM-R, Cinder R or Cinder RX from the Prototype family. These ten builds have distinct acceleration, handling, speed limits and nitro tuning. GT and Formula builds adapt credited model sources. The two closed-cockpit and two open-cockpit Prototype builds share original body profiles and use credited GT wheel geometry. Names and handling are fictional.

How many circuits and laps are there?

There are five circuits and every race lasts three laps. Harbor Flow is about 1.22 km per lap, Dockyard Technical about 1.53 km, Coast Run about 1.13 km, Summit Switchback about 1.66 km and Bay Grand Prix about 1.53 km. Each race starts with you and three rivals.

How do I use and recharge nitro?

Hold Shift on a keyboard or the Nitro touch pad to boost. Release the control to recharge while driving; clean drifts recharge it faster. Stock tanks range from 2.8 seconds in Apex Sprint to 3.8 seconds in Torque RS. Each Nitro upgrade adds 0.35 seconds, for 1.75 extra seconds at level 5, and improves thrust and recharge. Braking or holding the handbrake interrupts boost. After the tank empties, release the control before using it again.

Can I play Blacktop Bay on a phone?

Yes, on a phone browser that supports WebGL 2. Separate touch pads steer, brake, drift and activate nitro, and mobile races use landscape orientation. Rotate upright to pause; rotate back and choose Keep driving to resume. Graphics performance depends on the device and browser.

How do I finish a race and save my result?

Complete three laps in the correct direction through the ordered checkpoints. Your result shows finishing position, race time, best lap, drift points, resets and earned race credits; rivals receive times when they actually finish. Best time and drift score are stored locally for each car-and-circuit combination when browser storage is available. Runs longer than 15 minutes do not create records or earn credits.

Can I play offline?

No offline mode is provided. An internet connection is needed to load the game, and Blacktop Bay does not install an offline cache. Once loaded, the racing simulation runs in your browser.

How do I recover or pause?

Press R or select Reset car to return to your last valid road position. Press Esc or P, or use the pause icon, to pause. Switching away from the tab pauses an active race. Resetting does not refill nitro or award free lap progress.

Can I upgrade every car?

Yes. Each car has five purchasable levels for Engine, Tyres, Nitro and Handling. Open Garage, choose a car and open Upgrades. The five level costs are 200, 400, 700, 1100 and 1600 race credits per component. Engine improves acceleration, tyres improve grip and braking, handling improves steering response and stability, and nitro improves boost capacity, thrust and recharge. All four also raise the speed ceiling. Levels are saved locally for each car and apply to later races when browser storage is available.

Can I change my car’s colour and finish?

Yes. Open Garage, choose a car and select Paint. Choose one of 10 curated colours or Team original, then select Gloss, Metallic or Satin. All paint is free and changes appearance only. Choices are saved separately for each car in this browser when storage is available; otherwise they last for the current page session. Clearing site data removes them.

How do I earn race credits, and where is progress saved?

You start with 1200 shared race credits. A valid three-lap finish awards 900 credits for first, 650 for second, 500 for third or 350 for fourth, plus one credit per 20 drift points up to a 500-credit bonus. Each race pays once. Your balance and per-car upgrades stay in this browser when storage is available; they do not sync to other devices. Clearing race records keeps workshop progress, while clearing site data removes it. If storage is blocked, new credits and upgrades last only for the current page session.

Browser and privacy notes

Use a browser with WebGL 2 graphics enabled. The game needs an internet connection to load. Switching away pauses an active race; reduced-motion preferences remove decorative camera motion while retaining movement needed to drive. The speaker button controls sound.

See the privacy notice for local records, workshop progress, hosting and analytics choices. No game account is required.
