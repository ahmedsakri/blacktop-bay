How to complete a Blacktop Bay race

Pick a car and circuit, start from the grid, and complete three laps through the checkpoints. These six steps cover the controls and race finish.

  1. Choose a race car and optional upgrades

    Open Garage and choose one of 27 cars. All are available immediately. Open Performance to spend earned race credits on Engine, Tyres, Nitro or Handling, or start with the stock car.

  2. Choose a circuit and start

    Choose any of 34 circuits in the circuit browser. Start with Harbor Flow, or explore Breakwater Run, Copper Canyon, Cedar Ridge or Neon Freight. Select Race Now or Race This Car and wait for the shared countdown; all four drivers start together.

  3. Steer into the corner

    Acceleration is automatic. Hold either side of the steering thumbpad or drag for fine control, then release to center. You can also drag across the race view or use Left/Right or A/D. Compatible phones can enable optional tilt in Pause → Steering. Use measured steering through bends; Down/S is an optional keyboard brake that overrides acceleration and nitro.

  4. Drift through a corner

    Turn sharply at speed to drift automatically, then ease the steering back into line to bank a clean drift. Keyboard players can also hold Space for the optional handbrake. Contact can lose unbanked points.

  5. Use nitro on a clear straight

    Hold Shift or the Nitro button to boost. Nitro is the only on-screen pedal; acceleration is automatic. Release Nitro to recharge while driving, with faster recharge during clean drifts. Keyboard braking or the optional handbrake interrupts boost. Release an empty trigger before trying to boost again.

  6. Complete three laps and read the results

    Follow the circuit through its ordered checkpoints for three laps. The finish screen shows your position, time and earned race credits while remaining rivals continue to their own finish. Choose Race Again, Garage or Home to continue.

Dockyard Technical

Summit Switchback

Bay Grand Prix

Breakwater Run

Copper Canyon

Cedar Ridge

About 2.41 km per lap. Find a rhythm through cedar-lined bends, open the throttle on the forest straight, and sweep past timber lodges under the ridgeline.

Neon Freight

About 2.26 km per lap. A floodlit freight district with a long loading-yard straight, generous ninety-degree turns and a fast return past container stacks.

Your car. Five levels further.

Every car has five upgrade levels each for Engine, Tyres, Nitro and Handling. From Garage, choose a car and open Upgrades. Levels improve real driving performance and remain installed for later races when browser storage is available.

You start with 1200 race credits, shared across the garage. Each component’s five levels cost 200, 400, 700, 1100 and 1600 credits. All cars and circuits are available from the start; credits cannot be bought with real money.

A valid three-lap finish pays 900 / 650 / 500 / 350 credits for first through fourth place. Earn one extra credit for every 20 drift points, capped at 500 bonus credits per race. Each race pays once; a run longer than 15 minutes earns no credits.

Upgrade levels are separate for each car, and progress is saved in this browser rather than a player account. Clear race records keeps your workshop; clearing site data removes credits and upgrades. If storage is blocked, new progress lasts only for the current page session.

Contact and recovery

Light barrier scrapes preserve most forward momentum. A hard impact briefly reduces drive and interrupts nitro, and loses any unbanked drift points. If you remain pressed into a barrier while accelerating for about 2.4 seconds, the player car automatically returns to a clear point behind its last valid progress. A car far off the road recovers after about 0.9 seconds while acceleration is requested. Recovery may wait for a clear gap, rewinds any crossed checkpoint requirement and has a three-second cooldown. Holding the brake or handbrake does not trigger automatic recovery. R or Reset car remains available for a manual return to the last valid road position. Neither method grants lap progress or refills nitro; race time keeps running.

Race the field. Reach the finish.

Mira, Jax and Nova drive visible, solid cars. They negotiate corners, attempt passes and can make contact with your car. Complete three laps in the correct direction through the ordered checkpoints; shortcuts, travelling backwards and resetting do not award free laps.

Your results show position, race time, best lap, drift points and resets. Rivals still racing receive their actual times when they finish. Your fastest time and best drift score are saved locally for each car and circuit when browser storage is available; runs over 15 minutes do not create records or earn race credits.

Frequently asked questions

What is Blacktop Bay?

Blacktop Bay is a free single-player 3D browser racing game by AppsOverFlow. Choose from 27 artist-made manufacturer models across 16 brands. Race three opponents over three laps on any of 34 asphalt circuits, drift through corners and manage rechargeable nitro. Earn race credits to upgrade your cars.

Is Blacktop Bay free, and do I need an account?

Yes. Blacktop Bay is free to play without a game account or app installation. All 27 cars and all thirty-four circuits are available from the start. Optional car upgrades use earned race credits, with no real-money payments.

Is Blacktop Bay multiplayer?

No. One human player races three computer-controlled drivers: Mira, Jax and Nova. The four-car field is a single-player race, not online multiplayer or ghost playback.

How do I use and recharge nitro?

Acceleration is automatic. Hold Shift or the Nitro button to boost; Nitro is the only on-screen pedal. Release it to recharge while driving; clean drifts recharge it faster. Stock tanks range from 3.0 seconds in the Lotus Elise to 3.9 seconds in the Koenigsegg One:1 and Bugatti Veyron. Each Nitro upgrade adds 0.35 seconds, for 1.75 extra seconds at level 5, and improves thrust and recharge. Keyboard braking overrides acceleration and nitro, and holding the optional handbrake interrupts boost. After the tank empties, release the control before using it again.

Can I play Blacktop Bay on a phone?

Yes, on a phone browser that supports WebGL 2. Race in landscape with automatic acceleration. Hold either side of the steering thumbpad or drag to steer, then release to center. Compatible phones can optionally enable tilt in Pause → Steering; grant motion access if asked and recenter while holding the phone comfortably. Touch always works as an override, and missing sensor data falls back to touch. Turn sharply at speed to drift automatically and hold Nitro to boost. Rotate upright to pause; rotate back and choose Keep driving to resume. The menu and garage also work in portrait. Graphics performance depends on the device and browser.

How do I finish a race and save my result?

Complete three laps in the correct direction through the ordered checkpoints. Your result shows finishing position, race time, best lap, drift points, resets and earned race credits; rivals receive times when they actually finish. Best time and drift score are stored locally for each car-and-circuit combination when browser storage is available. Runs longer than 15 minutes do not create records or earn credits.

Can I play offline?

No offline mode is provided. An internet connection is needed to load the game, and Blacktop Bay does not install an offline cache. Once loaded, the racing simulation runs in your browser.

How do I recover or pause?

Press R or select Reset car to return to your last valid road position. If you remain stuck against a barrier while accelerating, or end up far off the road, automatic recovery can return you behind your validated progress after a short wait. It avoids nearby opponents and cannot add lap progress or refill nitro. Braking or holding the handbrake does not trigger automatic recovery. Press Esc or P, or use the pause icon, to pause. Switching away from the tab pauses an active race.

Can I upgrade every car?

Yes. Each car has five purchasable levels for Engine, Tyres, Nitro and Handling. Open Garage, choose a car and open Upgrades. The five level costs are 200, 400, 700, 1100 and 1600 race credits per component. Engine improves acceleration, tyres improve grip and braking, handling improves steering response and stability, and nitro improves boost capacity, thrust and recharge. All four also raise the speed ceiling. Levels are saved locally for each car and apply to later races when browser storage is available.

Can I change my car’s colour and finish?

Yes. Open Garage, choose a car and select Paint. Choose one of 10 curated colours or Team original, then select Gloss, Metallic or Satin. All paint is free and changes appearance only. Choices are saved separately for each car in this browser when storage is available; otherwise they last for the current page session. Clearing site data removes them.

Your balance and per-car upgrades stay in this browser when storage is available; they do not sync to other devices.
