# Additional recorded-audio attribution for public credits

Keep the existing four source entries. Add the entries below and update coverage to “all 33 cars, 14 banks”. All derivative banks were modified by AppsOverFlow: selected segments, mono conversion, 24 kHz resampling, filtering, DC/loop correction, overlap and amplitude normalization; in-game pitch, gain, tone and rev crossfades are authored. These are disclosed adaptations, not recordings of every exact represented car, and imply no recorder/manufacturer endorsement.

- **[Lamborghini Sounds](https://freesound.org/people/JaimeLopes/sounds/564375/)** by JaimeLopes — [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Lamborghini Huracán EVO Spyder V10. Family proxy for the game Huracán (variant unspecified), 2004 Gallardo and Audi R8/R8 LMS. No claim that the custom R8 uses this exact engine.
- **[Lambo idle and rev.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/cheesepuff/sounds/112075/)** by cheesepuff — [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Lamborghini Murciélago LP 670 SuperVeloce V12. Modern V12 family proxy for Aventador, Zonda, GMA T.50 and Enzo. Veyron uses a deliberately lower, darker multi-cylinder proxy; this is not a W16 recording.
- **[ferrari340america2.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/enginemusic/sounds/43483/)** by enginemusic — [CC-BY-3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Ferrari 340 America 4-litre V12. Classic V12 family proxy for Ferrari 250 GTO and Countach. The Testarossa uses a classic twelve-cylinder texture adaptation; not a recording of its flat-12.
- **[2002-Honda-F20C.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2002-Honda-F20C.ogg)** by Tyler Riddle — [CC-BY-3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). 2002 Honda F20C naturally aspirated inline-four. Naturally aspirated four-cylinder family proxy for Lotus Elise; not a Lotus or Toyota recording.
- **[audi a4 allroad 20TFSI engine rpm ramp fast mono.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Soundholder/sounds/425384/)** by Soundholder — [CC-BY-3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Audi A4 Allroad 2.0 TFSI turbo inline-four. Small turbo-engine texture proxy for BMW i8 (actual turbo inline-three hybrid) and Porsche 919 (actual turbo V4 hybrid), alongside original hybrid synthesis. Neither cylinder arrangement is asserted to match.
- **[RG Volvo Engine.wav](https://freesound.org/people/cmusounddesign/sounds/95838/)** by cmusounddesign — [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Volvo 2.4 turbo inline-five. Turbo five-cylinder family proxy for Audi Quattro rally; source is a Volvo road engine, not an Audi rally recording.

Inline-six family proxy for BMW M3 E46. Recording layer fades away at standstill because this is a driving take.
