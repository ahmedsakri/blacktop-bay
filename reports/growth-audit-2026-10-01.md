# Blacktop Bay growth audit — 1 October 2026

Blacktop Bay’s strongest next improvements are a clearer first race, a reason to race again, and broader distribution. This is a source review informed by browser-game platform guidance, not a finding from audience analytics or a promise of popularity.

The current game already offers 16 manufacturer car representations across 13 brands, 34 circuits, three AI opponents, earned upgrades, paint, synthesized driving audio, local records, touch/keyboard/optional tilt controls, crash recovery and consent-based analytics. The manufacturer-only update removed the fictional cars from selection and removed their original-model startup preload. The earlier 16.28 MiB original-mesh startup finding is therefore resolved; it must not be repeated as a current loading defect. Real-device performance and cold-load timings still need measurement.

1. **Make the first race easy to understand and finish.** The current Race Now action enters a full three-lap race; How to Race is optional text and illustrated cards. Add a skippable short beginner event with visual steering, drift and Nitro prompts, followed by the normal race. Observe first-time players and measure where they leave before expanding the tutorial. Evidence: [race entry](../src/main.js), [help panel](../src/race-dialogs.js), [three-lap rules](../src/physics.js). [Poki recommends safe introductory play and gradual visual instruction](https://developers.poki.com/guide/easy-access).

2. **Give each finish a clear next goal.** Results currently offer Race Again, Garage and Home. Credits and upgrades already exist, but there are no cups, track medals or suggested next events. Add a compact three-race cup, visible medal targets and a useful next-event action. Preserve the freely available car collection. Evaluate whether more players start a second race. [Results](../src/main.js) and [progression](../src/progression.js) provide the foundation. [Poki’s engagement guidance](https://developers.poki.com/guide/engagement) prioritizes satisfying short loops and clear goals.

3. **Keep races challenging and comparisons fair.** Opponent pace is fixed, rivals use stock tuning, and player upgrades affect performance. Introduce Rookie/Sport/Expert settings and a standardized stock-car time trial. Current personal records separate car and circuit but not upgrade level, so faster times can reflect both skill and upgrades. Separate comparable challenge records. Evidence: [rival pace](../src/rivals.js), [race construction](../src/physics.js), [record keys](../src/main.js).

4. **Make good results shareable.** Add a result card and a “Beat my time” link with track, car and common rules. Track links already work. Personal replay frames and ghost materials exist, but stored frames are not currently played back as a visible ghost. Finish personal-ghost racing before building a public leaderboard; public competitive results would need validation beyond editable local storage. Evidence: [saved frames](../src/storage.js), [race entry and results](../src/main.js).

5. **Measure and improve loading and mobile play.** The removed preload is an improvement, not proof of fast loading. Measure cold start, asset failures and sustained race performance on real iOS/Android devices and modest laptops. Mobile currently uses a fixed 1.5 pixel ratio with post-processing; add a usable low-quality option if measurements justify it. Physical-phone limitations are documented in the [earlier release report](manufacturer-circuits-controls-release.md). [Poki advises progressive loading](https://developers.poki.com/guide/easy-access).

6. **Measure the play funnel.** Existing consent-gated events cover race starts, completions, pauses and selections. Add load-ready/failure, first-run completion, replay and upgrade-purchase events within the existing privacy controls. Compare loading-to-play, first-finish and second-race rates before and after changes. No real retention figures were inspected for this audit. Evidence: [analytics allowlist](../src/analytics.js).

7. **Prepare distribution and communicate one compelling experience.** Build a portal-compatible edition and test its integration before submitting. Current Firebase headers block iframe embedding; root-absolute asset paths also require review for portal hosting. Pair truthful gameplay captures with clearly labelled promotional artwork, a readable thumbnail and a short drift/overtake/Nitro clip. SEO files already exist; the [recorded search-console observations](seo-review.md) do not establish indexing success. [CrazyGames requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/) cover relative paths, loading limits and SDK integration; [Poki’s thumbnail guidance](https://developers.poki.com/guide/game-thumbnail) emphasizes clear imagery that matches player expectations.

Start with the first-race experience and measurement, then add compact goals and friend challenges. Treat these as testable priorities, and use observed player behavior to decide the next change.
