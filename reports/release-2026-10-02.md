# Blacktop Bay — 2 October 2026 release

## Scope

- 33 individually credited manufacturer cars across 16 brands; eight-car races with seven independently prepared opponents.
- Dedicated `/cars/`, `/cars/<id>/`, `/circuits/` and `/circuits/<id>/` URLs, with static crawlable metadata and sitemap entries.
- Circuit race, solo Time attack and Three-race tour. Records are scoped by circuit, vehicle, mode and difficulty. Credit receipts prevent duplicate result awards; storage failures retain the current state and report the failure.
- Current racing-menu hierarchy across the lobby, car collection, circuit selection and 3D garage: compact resource rail, actual car presentation, clear primary action and consistent illustrated icons. New violet studio mural uses nine static draw calls, derivative-filtered graphics and neutral car lighting.
- Charge-proportional trapezoid Nitro display with yellow charge, blue active boost and accessible meter status. No invented extra boost modes.
- Directional collision feedback, bounded impact camera response, improved wall recovery, validated progress checks and responsive drag steering.
- Liquid Lines is the only lobby soundtrack. Previous choices migrate without changing saved mute or independent music/game volumes. Engine voices are original synthesis, not recordings of manufacturer engines.
- AppsOverFlow listing updated to the same car, circuit and field counts and links.

## Limits

Phone checks use browser viewport/touch emulation. Physical iPhone/Android sensor permissions, actual gyroscope readings, two-finger hardware input and sustained mobile thermal performance were not verified in this run. The checks do not establish zero possible defects or pixel-identical equivalence to another game.
