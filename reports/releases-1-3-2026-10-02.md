## Release 1 — dependable play, controls and saves

- **Lobby and dialogs:** race-panel rows retain their natural height, short desktop screens use a horizontal circuit card, and constrained panels scroll without shrinking the circuit card underneath another control. Long names and goals wrap; primary actions retain at least 44 CSS px targets. Portrait scrolling content has separate space above fixed navigation. Nested dialog Back restores the parent launcher, including a rebuilt button inside a collapsed disclosure; final close returns to the original available opener. Keyboard trapping includes disclosure summaries.
- **Race timing:** rendered frames and simulation time are separated. Ordinary elapsed time is fed to the existing 120 Hz physics accumulator in chunks no larger than 1/30 second. Tests compare 30/60/120 Hz schedules and repeated 100–250 ms stalls. An active gap longer than 300 ms contributes no simulation time and requests a visible pause. Countdown overflow is carried into the first racing step. Hidden tabs, graphics interruption and explicit pause do not add driving time; returning requires the appropriate player action.
- **Graphics recovery:** context loss clears held input, holds the run, stops active audio demand and shows recovery status. World/garage reflection resources, shadows and postprocessing are rebuilt, then programs compiled. Generation checks reject obsolete attempts after each asynchronous boundary; another loss invalidates an in-flight restore. Car selection keeps the previous model if graphics preparation is interrupted. The recovery UI offers retry, backup and reload if restoration fails. Successful recovery returns to the relevant view; an interrupted race remains paused.
- **Records:** a personal best is called saved only after the write verifies by exact read-back. Failed or unverifiable writes retain a scoped session candidate; changing views or completing a slower run cannot erase it. Session-only candidates are lost on reload or tab/process discard unless successfully retried or exported. Results show a session-only message and Retry save / Export unsaved records. Exported record files can be validated and merged through Save backup, preserving faster existing times and unrelated progress. Full portable backups also include pending session records. Authentication data and analytics consent remain excluded.
- **Tour continuation:** the career and next-round preferences are staged together behind a durable journal, verified before the visible selection changes, and rolled back on failure. Once a journal is known, denied storage keeps recovery blocking until the saved state can be established. Actual finish times stay intact; leaving a completed round classifies unfinished rivals as DNF with zero points. No opponent finish time is invented.
- **Driving:** manual reset uses legal-road/obstacle candidates behind validated progress, checks visible-body clearance and vertical traffic separation, and does nothing if no safe candidate exists. It neither awards progress nor refills Nitro. In toggle mode the first tap starts Nitro, a tap in a valid Burst/Perfect window upgrades and keeps the boost active, and a tap outside that window or after the upgrade stops it. Physical press identifiers prevent a held input or brake release from becoming a second tap. Help, settings and school instructions reflect this behavior.

Implementation details: [UI evidence](ui-reliability-2026-10-02.md), [driving/audio/record contract](../docs/driving-audio-records.md). The existing 18-event campaign, car mastery, per-circuit setups, original asset attribution and privacy choices are preserved.

This trial trades larger delivery files for approximately 75% smaller texture block storage at the stated 8bpp target. Driver overhead and block rounding are unmeasured. It does not claim faster downloads or catalogue-wide GPU compression.

Automatic quality now includes repeated severe intervals above 150 ms instead of discarding them. At least four consecutive samples and two seconds of bounded pressure trigger a reduction, with a four-second cooldown; each sample contributes at most 500 ms. Severe intervals also enter ordinary p75 windows, so alternating stalls remain visible. A separate interruption path covers the integration where a gap above 300 ms pauses play and ordinary frame sampling resets: three active interruptions within a rolling 30-second window lower Auto by one level. A four-second wall-time cooldown prevents repeated changes, pause resets retain only this bounded history, and the first isolated interruption does nothing. This path changes rendering work without advancing driving time. Manual quality remains authoritative. A single hitch does not force a downgrade. Intentional battery-saver cadence, menus, hidden tabs and loading are excluded by the caller. Existing mobile sharpness profiles and the 1× automatic floor remain. Mobile render scheduling remains 30 Hz for menu/garage, 15 Hz paused, and at most 60 Hz racing, or 30 Hz racing with Battery saver. These scheduling limits do not establish delivered FPS or reduced temperature.
