# Blacktop Bay release — 1 October 2026

## Delivered

- Twenty race builds: seven GT, four Formula and nine prototype builds. Six additions include open-cockpit, long-tail, sprint, canopy, offset-cockpit and finned silhouettes. Existing GT source geometry and saved progress are preserved. These are adaptations of credited meshes and original bodywork, not twenty independently sourced models.
- Thirty circuits: five existing courses, 23 current-calendar venue adaptations and two original-calendar bonus venues. Map-derived outlines are scaled and softened for arcade driving; Suzuka's crossing is deliberately unrolled. Research and full MIT attribution are retained.
- Searchable car catalogue with family filters, favourites, sorting by upgraded ratings and comparison; searchable regional circuit atlas. Every car remains immediately available.
- Exact yellow/violet palette, dark base/navy panels, restrained gold rewards, illustrated SVG action frames/icons, refreshed vector logo/loader, racing type and consistent touch targets. Car paint colours remain independent of interface colours.
- Revised help, pause, results, workshop and paint controls; a confirmation before clearing race records; three synthesized engine characters with shifts, load and Nitro response.
- Mandatory UI quality standard in `docs/ui-quality-standard.md` and linked repository instructions.

## Browser review

Reviewed in the Codex Chromium browser, using the actual local production build. Sizes covered across the change: 1280×800, 390×844, 375×667, 320×640, 844×390 and 568×320. Viewport checks are not a substitute for testing on every physical phone or Safari version.

Exercised the title screen, garage, car selection, favourite persistence after reload, search/filter/sort, keyboard focus traversal, circuit switching, workshop purchase, paint persistence, rotation gate, live race start/automatic acceleration, pause and help. A Vector F engine upgrade changed the displayed speed from 167 to 173 km/h and deducted 200 credits; Corsa Red/Satin remained after reload. The mobile HUD shows Nitro as the only touch driving button. Clearing records was cancelled; QA did not erase records.

Found and fixed during review: a covered help action at compact landscape height; an oversized inherited garage label; a wrapped Race Now label; mismatched paint button styling; unnecessary emphasis on Performance; and sky clipping on the largest venue. Post-fix representative views have no known blocked primary actions or action overlaps.

Race-results layout was checked separately at 320 px portrait and 844×390 landscape with `reports/ui-overlay-review.html`. This local fixture is not shipped and is not represented as an actual user-driven completed race. Automated simulation tests exercise full races and results data.

Primary text/action contrast ratios: yellow on base 17.93:1; white on base 20.27:1; white on navy 18.08:1; white on violet 4.64:1. Destructive controls use white text on a dark surface with a magenta border; white-on-magenta small text is avoided. These checks do not claim a complete accessibility certification.

## Automated checks

Firebase's required predeploy runs the complete Node test suite and production build. Tests cover real three-lap circuit simulations, all car builds, saved progression/paint, audio routing, touch-input cancellation and two-finger ownership, collection filters, results and venue geometry. Final predeploy result: **298 tests passed, zero failures**, in 53.1 seconds; production build passed. Firebase released 41 files to https://blacktop-bay.web.app/. Nine production pages/assets (including the main HTML, hashed CSS/JavaScript, sprite, button artwork, logo and information pages) returned HTTP 200 and matched the local production build byte for byte.

The bundle retains the existing renderer startup design; Vite reports a large JavaScript chunk warning. No new runtime dependency was added. Native phone performance and all GPU/browser combinations remain outside this review's coverage.

AppsOverFlow now describes twenty builds and thirty circuits, and replaces typographic action glyphs with a shared inline SVG icon system on its homepage, guides, sharing, contact links and footer. The hosted contact form/backend was not changed. Its release checks passed: 3,037 static assertions, 541 interaction assertions, 53 unit tests, 22 contact backend mocks and 148 contact client/iframe checks. Published commit: `4b6f174`.
